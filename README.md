1. Getting started
Kura is a home screen for your notebook. Write the moment a thought arrives, bring in pages from your
paper notebook, sort everything into DROP, STOCK and MAKE, plan TODAY, and keep longer,
cleaned-up notes in the Notebook. Everything is saved on this phone.
Set up in about five minutes
1. Tap the gear (Settings).
2. Obsidian vault (optional): type your vault name exactly as Obsidian shows it, then tap Save.
Obsidian must be installed on this phone.
3. AI (optional but recommended): choose GEMINI · FREE, get a free key at aistudio.google.com (Get
API key -> Create API key), paste it and tap Save and test.
4. Your field: type something like "biomedical engineering: catheter electrodes, bioimpedance". The
notebook uses it to understand technical notes.
5. 6. Close Settings, try the example notes on the home screen, then tap Clear examples in the banner.
Google Tasks (optional): see the Google Tasks chapter.
Everything works without an AI key. Kura then sorts with simple rules on your phone, and you bring in
paper pages by pasting text from Google Lens.

9. Brain Camera and pasting a page
With an AI key
1. 2. 3. Tap the camera in the pad, or the picture button to use a photo you already took.
Photograph one page (or a two-page spread) in good light, filling the frame.
Kura reads your handwriting and your symbols and splits the page into notes. This takes 10 to 40
seconds.
4. Check each note: fix the text, change DROP / STOCK / MAKE, tap Today, untick lines you don't
want. Set the category and project for the page.
5. Tick Keep the photo to keep it on the phone with the notes. Send to Obsidian sends the page too.
Then tap Add.
Without a key (free)
1. 2. 3. Tap the camera button: it opens Paste a page.
Open Google Lens (or the camera app), point it at the page, tap Text -> Select all -> Copy.
Paste into Kura and tap Split into notes. Each line becomes a note, and a symbol at the start of a
line becomes a tag.
Your symbols
Tap the Edit tile to change symbol marks and names, and describe how you draw each one (for
example "a small box before the line"). Brain Camera uses that description to spot them. You can have
up to 8.
Samsung photos that won't open: turn off High efficiency pictures in the camera settings.

10. The Notebook and the Bank
The Notebook is where longer notes live. It has four tabs: Bank, Projects, Glossary and Ask.
• The Bank holds every notebook note and quick note, newest first.
• Filter with the All / Work / Personal / Fun chips, and with All · Notebook · Quick notes. Search covers
titles, text and tags.
• Tap a notebook note to read it. Tap a quick note to open its sheet.
• On the cover screen, <- takes you back from a note to the list.
• The bottom of the list has NotebookLM (send a pack) and Save an answer.
11. Writing notebook notes
1. Tap + New. Pick a template if you like: Experiment, Paper, Meeting, Daily log or Blank. It fills in the
headings.
2. Write freely. Shorthand, half-sentences and symbols are fine. Transcribe a page turns a photo of
handwriting into text (AI), and Dictate types what you say.
3. Tap Comprehend (AI) to get a clean note. Without a key it says Tidy (no AI), which keeps your
words and finds tasks and questions from your symbols. Save as written skips both.
4. Check what Kura understood: title, category and project (suggested, tap to change), summary, key
points, the cleaned note, Technical context, tasks and open questions.
5. Untick any task or question you don't want. Tap Today on a task to plan it. Then tap Save note.
Tasks
Open questions
Go to STOCK with ? Unresolved.
Go to MAKE, or TODAY if you tapped Today. Dates in them ("by fri") are planned.
Technical context
AI explanations of the technical terms, from textbook knowledge. They are added to the Glossary.
Afterwards
In a saved note: change its category or project at the top, Edit it, Re-comprehend it, Share it, send a
NotebookLM pack, Send to Obsidian, or Delete it (with Undo). Tasks the note created follow its category
and project.
The AI is told never to invent results, numbers or citations. The Paper template keeps your citation
exactly as you wrote it.
12. The Glossary
• Every term in a note's Technical context is saved automatically, sorted A to Z and linked to the notes
that use it.
• Different spellings of one term are merged, like "EIS" and "Electrochemical impedance spectroscopy
(EIS)".
• Tap a term in a note to jump to it.
• Edit a definition to make it yours. AI never overwrites it after that.
• Delete a term and Kura won't add it again.
• Send to Obsidian (bottom of the list) writes one Glossary note. It shows whether Obsidian is up to
date.

1. 2. 3. Notebook -> Projects -> type a name at the bottom -> Add.
From then on, #name in any quick note files it there, e.g. #aurora.
You can also pick a project in a task's sheet, when reviewing a notebook note, on a Brain Camera
page, or for something shared into Kura.
A project page shows
• Progress (tasks done out of all tasks).
• The Next action, with + Today, > Focus and Done buttons.
• A box to add tasks (dates work there).
• Open tasks, open questions, notebook notes (+ New note here), kept notes and done tasks.
Archive hides a finished project. Rename, Share a summary, send a NotebookLM pack, or Delete it.
Deleting keeps its notes and tasks.
14. Ask your notes
• Notebook -> Ask. Type a question like "what did I find about foam impedance?".
• Answers come from your notebook notes, quick notes and glossary. Each fact is cited, and you can
tap the chips to open the source.
• If your notes don't cover it, Kura says so. Anything it adds from general knowledge starts with
"General knowledge:".
• The category chips limit the question to Work, Personal or Fun.
• Without an AI key, Ask lists the notes that best match your question.
15. Plan my day
... More -> Plan my day suggests 3 to 5 tasks to add to TODAY, each with a reason.
• Suggestions are based on due dates, Important marks, project next actions, how long a task has
waited, and steps already started.
• Ask AI re-plans with AI, taking into account what is already in TODAY.
• Tick the ones you want and tap Add to TODAY. Undo is available.
• Long-press the Kura icon -> Plan my day to jump straight here.

21. Google Tasks
Kura syncs both ways with a Google Tasks list (normally called "Kura") through a small script in your
own Google account. It is free.
From Kura
Tasks in TODAY or with a date (and MAKE tasks, if you turn that on): new, edited, done and deleted
From Google Tasks
New tasks, ticks, date changes and deletions come back into Kura
When
When you open Kura, after changes, and every 5 minutes while it is open
Set up
1. Settings -> Google Tasks -> Copy bridge script.
2. On a computer, go to script.google.com while signed in to your personal Google account -> New
project. Paste the script over everything.
3. 4. Under Services, add Tasks (Google Tasks API).
Deploy -> New deployment -> Web app. Set Execute as: Me, Who has access: Anyone. Authorise
it.
5. Copy the Web app URL that ends in /exec, paste it into Kura and tap Save and test.
If testing fails with 404, the deployment isn't open to "Anyone", or it was made with a school or work
account that doesn't allow that. Edit the deployment ((edit) -> New version), or make it with your
personal Gmail. Check connections (in Settings) explains what's wrong.
22. NotebookLM
Send notes to NotebookLM
1. 2. Tap NotebookLM in the Bank, on a project page, on a note, or in ... More.
Choose the notes (all, a category, a project or one note) and the dates. Tick what to include:
Technical context, tasks and questions, your original words.
3. Tap Share PDF, choose NotebookLM, then pick a notebook. It becomes a source you can ask
questions of.
• NotebookLM keeps each PDF as a separate source. After the first pack, pick New or changed since
last pack, or delete the old source in NotebookLM.
• Download saves the PDF to Downloads. Copy as text is for NotebookLM's "Copied text" source, and
keeps every symbol exactly.
• The PDF uses standard fonts: Ohm becomes "Ohm", -> becomes "->" and Greek letters are spelled
out. µ and ° stay.
Save NotebookLM answers in Kura
1. 2. 3. In NotebookLM, copy an answer (or share it to Kura).
In Kura: Bank -> Save an answer (or pick NotebookLM answer in the share sheet).
Add the question and the notebook name if you like, then Save as note. It is tagged #notebooklm
and keeps its [1] [2] citations. Re-comprehend pulls out tasks and glossary terms.

23. Sharing and shortcuts
Share into Kura
In Chrome, NotebookLM or any app, tap Share -> Kura. Save it as a quick note, a task, a Paper note
(the citation is filled in) or a notebook note, and add it to a project.
Long-press the Kura icon
Shortcuts for New note, Scan a page, Focus timer and Plan my day.
Kura appears in the share sheet, and the shortcuts appear on the icon, after Chrome refreshes the
installed app. That usually happens on its own within a day.
Share out
Tasks, notebook notes and projects have a Share button. It uses the phone's share sheet, or copies the
text when sharing isn't available.
24. AI keys, costs and privacy
Gemini · free
Key from aistudio.google.com. No card. Daily limits apply, and Google may use free-tier content to
improve its products.
Claude · paid
Your Anthropic API key, billed per use. Separate from a Claude subscription.
• Kura picks models automatically when you save the key: a stronger one for reading pages, a faster
one for sorting.
• AI is only used when you use an AI feature (AUTO sort, Brain Camera, Comprehend, Transcribe,
Ask, weekly summary, Plan with AI). Only the text or photo involved is sent.
• If the AI is unavailable or out of quota, Kura falls back to rules on the phone and tells you why.
• The key stays on this phone. Remove key deletes it.
25. Sounds, vibration and look
• 8-bit sounds (Settings -> Effects, or ... More -> Sound): a laser when you finish a task, a chime when
focus ends, a jingle for trophies. They play through media volume even on silent, so they start off.
• Vibration is on by default.
• Kura follows your phone's light or dark mode, and the status bar matches it.
• Work, Personal and Fun use blue, aqua and orange, and are always labelled with text too.
26. Backup, updates and installing
Your data
Notes, the notebook, the glossary, projects, stats and settings are stored in Chrome's storage for Kura
on this phone. Nothing goes to a Kura server; there isn't one.
• Settings -> Backup -> Export backup saves a .json file. Import backup adds it back on any phone.
Photos are not included.
• Clearing Chrome's data for the Kura site, or uninstalling the app, can erase your notes. Export first.

27. Troubleshooting
AI v shows a problem
Tap it. Usually the free quota ran out (try later) or the key was pasted incorrectly (save it again).
Obsidian didn't open
Check the vault name matches exactly, and that Obsidian is installed with that vault opened once.
Google Tasks 404 / not connected
See the Google Tasks chapter: the deployment must be a Web app with access set to Anyone, using its
/exec URL.
Voice does nothing
Allow the microphone for the Kura site in Chrome and check you are online.
Kura is missing from the share sheet
Chrome refreshes the installed app within about a day. Reinstalling makes it immediate, but export a
backup first.
The camera photo won't open
Turn off High efficiency pictures in the Samsung camera settings.
The top bar is white
Reinstall Kura from Chrome (export a backup first).
Notes vanished after reinstalling
Import your latest backup: Settings -> Backup -> Import backup.
Focus timer finished while away
It catches up when you open Kura: the minutes are logged and the break starts then.
Settings -> Check connections tests AI, Google Tasks and Obsidian and says what to fix. Copy report
gives you something to paste when asking for help.
